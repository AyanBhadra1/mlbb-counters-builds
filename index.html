When I Update This

No auto-feeds or bots here — I check everything myself, by hand, so what you're reading is what I'd actually use in my own ranked games.

Hey, thanks for stopping by — really glad you're here.

I put this site together for players like us who just want a quick, honest answer before a match — who counters who, what to build, and what's actually working this patch — without digging through five different places.

Every Thursday · 4:00 PM · Dhaka time (GMT+6)

That's my fixed check-in time, every single week. Thursday is when the new patch notes and weekly changes usually land, so at 4:00 PM on Thursdays I go through the latest notes, look at how the win and pick rates have moved, and refresh the counters, builds and graphs on this page if anything has changed.

If there's a new patch, you'll see the updates here after that Thursday check. If nothing big dropped that week, I still do the check — I just leave things as they are rather than changing numbers for the sake of it.

So if you're checking back for fresh info, Thursday after 4:00 PM Dhaka time is the best time to come back. Thanks again for visiting and for trusting this little guide — good luck in your next match, hope you get that win streak going.

— Xelrnik

Host It Yourself

This site is plain static files — no build step, no server, nothing tied to Muse. To put it on your own free GitHub Pages hosting: 1) create a GitHub repo, 2) upload index.html and the assets folder to the repo root, 3) open Settings → Pages → “Deploy from a branch” → main / (root) → Save. Your site goes live at your-username.github.io/repo-name — share that link with friends, and the future phone app can open the exact same page. To update it for a new patch, just replace index.html in the repo.
